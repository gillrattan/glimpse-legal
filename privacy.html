Glimpse on TV Privacy Policy

Effective 10/29/2026 · Applies to the Glimpse on TV app for iPhone

The short version

  • Your photos and faces never leave your phone. Glimpse never uploads them.
  • Slideshows, face and pet recognition are all made on your phone.
  • Slideshows reach your TV over your home Wi-Fi, not over the internet.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we do not collect it.

Glimpse on TV ("Glimpse", "the app", "we") is made by Rattan Gill. This policy explains what the app does with your information. If something here is unclear, write to us at gillrattan98@yahoo.com.

1. Your photos and videos

Glimpse asks for access to your photo library so you can pick pictures for slideshows. Everything Glimpse does with them happens on your phone:

  • Slideshow videos are built on your phone and kept in the app's own storage until you delete them. Nothing deletes them for you.
  • Glimpse looks for faces, and for dogs and cats, so you can find people and pets in your photos. This happens on your phone. The results (a private index of where faces and pets appear, and any names you give people) stay on your phone.
  • Your photos, videos, faces and the names you give people are never sent to us or to any other company.

You can withdraw photo access at any time in iPhone Settings. Deleting the app deletes the libraries, slideshow videos and the face and pet index it keeps.

2. Showing slideshows on your TV

To play a slideshow on a TV, Glimpse briefly makes the finished video available from your phone to devices on the same Wi-Fi network, and your TV fetches it from there. The video does not travel over the internet. This is why Glimpse asks for access to your local network and uses your Wi-Fi connection to find TVs.

Casting uses Google Cast and AirPlay, which are services built into your TV, your phone and Apple's and Google's software. Those parts are run by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies, and we do not receive anything from them. Google's Cast software, which Glimpse includes, may send Google anonymous information about how casting is working, such as finding and connecting to cast devices and basic device details. Google describes this as not linked to you and not shared with third parties, and it cannot be switched off from within Glimpse. See Google's Privacy Policy. It does not include your photos or videos.

3. Places and maps

To turn photo locations into place names (for example "Lisbon") and to find photos from a place you type in, Glimpse uses Apple's location services on your phone. This sends coordinates, or the place name you typed, to Apple. Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see these locations.

4. Glimpse Pro and purchases

Glimpse is free to use with limits. Glimpse Pro is an optional subscription that removes them.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card, name, email or Apple ID.
  • To confirm whether Pro is active, Glimpse uses a subscription service called RevenueCat. It receives a random, anonymous app identifier, your purchase receipt and subscription status, and basic technical details such as the app version, device type and your IP address. It is not given your photos, your name or your email.

5. App updates

When you open Glimpse, it may check for a fix to the app's screens using Expo's update service (Expo's Privacy Policy). The check shares only the app version and platform, along with the technical details any internet request includes, such as your IP address.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Glimpse uses them on your phone only, for example to tell you a slideshow is ready. These are created by the app on your phone and do not use a push notification server.

7. Problem reports

If the app runs into a problem, it keeps a short list of recent problems on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can look at the list in Settings and choose whether to share it with us, for example to get help. If you share it, we use it only to find and fix the problem.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade information about you.
  • We do not show ads or track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not use your photos or faces to train any AI model.

10. Children

Glimpse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Your choices and rights

Because your photos and the face and pet index stay on your phone, you control them directly: delete a library, delete slideshow videos, or delete the app. If you live somewhere with privacy rights (such as the EU, the UK or California), you may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. The only information we could hold is a message you send us, and we will delete it if you ask. For information held by Apple, RevenueCat or Expo, contact them using the links above.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information in a meaningful way, we will also say so in the app.
